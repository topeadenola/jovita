Photography contract template

A short, clear contract for portrait sessions. Copy it, change the parts in brackets, and send it to your client.

Here is a short, plain-English photography contract you can copy and use for portrait sessions. Change the parts in brackets for each client. It covers the fee and deposit, cancellations, late arrival, copyright and how you can use the photos.

This is a starting point, not legal advice. Laws differ by country, so have a lawyer check it before you rely on it.

The template

Portrait Photography Agreement This agreement is made on [Today's date] between [Client name], [Client address] ("the Client"), and [Your studio name] ("the Photographer"). 1. The session The Photographer will photograph the Client's [Session name] on [Session date] at [Session location]. The Photographer is the only photographer for this session unless both agree otherwise in writing. 2. Fee and payment The total fee is [Fee]. A non-refundable [70%] deposit is due when this agreement is signed and secures the date. The remaining [30%] is due on the day of the shoot. 3. Extra costs If the session is outside [Your city], the Client covers reasonable travel costs. The Client also pays for anything extra the session needs, such as permits, special equipment or sets. 4. Cancelling or rescheduling If the Client cancels, the deposit is not refunded. If the Client reschedules with at least [5] days' notice, the deposit moves to the new date. If the Photographer has to cancel, the Client gets the full deposit back or a new date. 5. Late arrival Time lost when the Client arrives late comes off the session, with no refund. 6. Style The Client has seen the Photographer's portfolio and expects a similar style. The Photographer will work in that style but cannot guarantee specific results. 7. Copyright and use The Photographer keeps the copyright. The Client may print and share the images for personal use, including on social media, but may not sell them or use them commercially without written permission. The Photographer may use the images in their portfolio, website and social media unless the Client asks otherwise in writing. 8. Things outside our control The Photographer is not responsible for things outside their control that affect the session, such as weather or illness. 9. Length of this agreement This agreement lasts until the fee is paid in full and the final images are delivered. It is the whole agreement for this session and replaces anything agreed before. Signed by the Client: ______________________ Date: __________ Signed by the Photographer: ________________ Date: __________

What each part is for

  • Fee and payment: a deposit that secures the date protects you from no-shows. Many photographers ask for 50 to 70%.
  • Cancelling or rescheduling: says what happens to the deposit, so there is no argument later.
  • Copyright and use: you keep the copyright, the client can print and share for personal use, and you can show the work in your portfolio.
  • Things outside our control: covers weather, illness and other things nobody can plan for.
