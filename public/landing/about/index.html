About Jovita

No PA. No operations manager.
Jovita's got you.

Jovita is the friendly business assistant for photographers, videographers, makeup artists and content creators. Clients, bookings, quotes, invoices, contracts and galleries live in one calm place, so you can spend your time on the work you love.

Why we built Jovita

Most creatives run their business on their own. The bookings come in through DMs and emails, the deposits are tracked in a notebook, contracts are sent as PDFs, and galleries go out through yet another app. Every hour spent chasing a payment is an hour not spent creating.

Big studios solve this by hiring a PA and an operations manager. Jovita gives a solo creative that same help, without the salary: it chases payments, sends contracts, books shoots, delivers galleries, drafts replies in your voice and keeps your books.

What makes Jovita different

Galleries and studio in one app

Quotes, contracts, payments and beautiful client galleries together, instead of four tools that don't talk to each other.

Built for how you get paid

Paystack, Flutterwave, PayPal and bank transfer, in naira, pounds, dollars, euros and CFA francs. Money goes straight to you.

Ask Jovita

An assistant that knows your studio. Ask who owes you, create an invoice or draft a reply. It only acts when you ask.

Made for what you do

Tell Jovita your craft and it sets up packages, contracts and emails that fit, from weddings to makeup bookings.

Reminders on by default

Friendly payment reminders go out for you, so you never have to send the awkward message again.

Speaks your language

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Swahili, for you and for your clients.

Who we're for

Jovita is built first for creatives in Africa, the United Kingdom and the United States, starting with photographers, videographers, makeup artists and content creators. Designers, stylists and other creatives are next.

Our promise

  • Your clients are yours. We never contact your clients for our own marketing, and we never sell your data.
  • You stay in control. Jovita never sends a message or touches money without you saying so.
  • Joy over admin. Every screen is designed to be calm, clear and quick, on your laptop or your phone.

The team

Jovita was founded by Tope Adenola and is built by a small, independent team that cares a lot about creatives and the craft of good software.
