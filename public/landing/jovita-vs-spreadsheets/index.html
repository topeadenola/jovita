Jovita vs spreadsheets

Manage your photography clients without spreadsheets

Spreadsheets are free, but chasing payments, retyping names and juggling apps costs you hours every week. Here's how a studio app compares.

Most photographers start the same way: a spreadsheet for bookings, a notebook for deposits, WhatsApp for everything else, contracts as PDFs, and galleries through WeTransfer or Google Drive. It works, until you have more than a few clients a month.

Where spreadsheets start to hurt

  • Chasing payments. You have to remember who owes what, then send the awkward "just checking in" message yourself.
  • Copying the same details everywhere. The client's name goes into the sheet, the invoice, the contract and the email, and one typo means a wrong contract.
  • Nothing talks to anything. The sheet doesn't know the contract was signed or the deposit landed, so you check three apps to answer one question.
  • It looks less professional. A PDF on WhatsApp is harder to sign, pay and keep than one tidy page with everything in it.

When a spreadsheet is still fine

If you do one or two shoots a month and your clients pay up front, a simple sheet may be all you need. Once you are sending quotes, contracts and invoices every week, or you have ever lost track of a deposit, a tool built for the job will save you time and money.

Moving from a spreadsheet to Jovita

  1. Export your client sheet as a CSV (in Google Sheets: File › Download › CSV).
  2. In Jovita, go to Clients › Import from file and drop it in. Jovita matches the columns for you, including name, email, phone, company, city and address.
  3. Add your packages and your contract once in Settings › Templates. From then on, every client gets them filled in.

Yes. Export it as a CSV and use Clients › Import from file. Jovita matches the columns for you and skips anyone already added.
