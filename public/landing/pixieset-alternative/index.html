Pixieset alternative

A Pixieset alternative built for African creatives

Quotes, contracts, invoices, payments and client galleries in one app, priced in naira and paid with Paystack, Flutterwave or bank transfer.

Pixieset is a popular, polished platform for client galleries, with a print store, a website builder and a studio manager. If it works for you, great. But many photographers, especially in Nigeria and across Africa, look for an alternative because of price in dollars, payment options, or because they want everything in one simpler app. Here is an honest comparison.

Switching from Pixieset

  1. Export your contacts from Pixieset (Contacts › Actions › Export Contacts).
  2. In Jovita, go to Clients › Import from file. Pixieset's columns, including Lead or Client, are picked up automatically.
  3. Add your packages and contract once, then send your next quote from Jovita.

Questions

Is Jovita cheaper than Pixieset?

For most solo creatives, yes. Jovita has a free plan, and Pro is $12 (₦15,000) a month for quotes, contracts, invoices, payments and galleries together, while Pixieset's Suite starts at $35 a month. Prices change, so check both sites.

Can I move my clients from Pixieset to Jovita?

Yes. Export your contacts from Pixieset as a CSV and import the file in Jovita under Clients › Import from file.

Does Jovita take Paystack and Flutterwave?

Yes. Clients can pay with Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe or bank transfer, in naira and other currencies.
