Rules

How Jo behaves

  • The chat icon staysAsk Jovita keeps its own "Typing to Smile" chat icon. Jo never replaces it.
  • One Jo per screenNever more than one, and never next to the logo.
  • Creatives onlyJo stays off client galleries, invoices and emails. The creative's brand leads there.
  • React, don't performEach animation plays once, at a real moment. Only the thinking dots loop while work is happening.
  • Never on bad money newsOverdue invoices and failed payments get plain words, not a cute face.
  • Small in the app64 to 120 px in the product. Big only in marketing, merch and the brand film.
  • Calm for reduced motionIf the device asks for less motion, Jo holds still and just smiles.
