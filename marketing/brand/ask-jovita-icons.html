Five icons for Ask Jovita

Each one is built from the smile mark and moves like someone who has your back: it pops, winks, nods or lights up. Pick one and it goes into the brand guidelines and the dashboard, pinned bottom-right.

In the product each icon plays its animation once when the dashboard opens, again on hover, and when Ask Jovita finishes a task. It never loops while you work. These previews loop so you can compare them.
