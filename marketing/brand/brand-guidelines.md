**Run your creative business with joy.**

## 01 · Our story
**Business, made joyful.**
Jovita comes from *jovial*: cheerful, warm, good company. It is the friendly studio manager for photographers, videographers, makeup artists, stylists and designers.
Creatives start a business to make beautiful work, not to chase invoices. Jovita handles clients, bookings, contracts and payments in one place, and tells you what needs doing today.

### Brand promise — four things we always deliver
1. **One place, not six apps** — one sidebar, one settings page, one brand setup.
2. **It tells you what's next** — every screen leads with what needs action today.
3. **It gets you paid** — reminders on by default. Naira, pounds and dollars.
4. **Clients love it too** — one link to sign, pay and see their photos.

### Who we serve — independent creatives and small studios
- **Creatives:** photographers, videographers, makeup artists, stylists, designers. Visual, busy, often running the business from a phone.
- **Markets:** Africa (₦ GH₵ KSh), United Kingdom (£), United States ($). Never assume one currency, one payment method or one date format.

## 02 · Logo
The primary logo (smile mark + lowercase wordmark). Use it wherever the brand appears for the first time.

### A j that smiles back
- **Two dots** — two eyes: the creative and the client, side by side.
- **The marigold hook** — the tail of the j is a smile. It is the only part that is always marigold.
- **Lowercase wordmark** — Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, always lowercase. Warm and approachable.

### Logo versions
| Version | Use on |
| --- | --- |
| Primary | Paper, white and light photos |
| Reversed | Plum, dark backgrounds and dark photos |
| One-colour mark | Marigold grounds, stamps, embossing |
| App icon | App stores, favicon, social avatar |

### Clear space & size
- Minimum clear space: **two eye-dot heights** on every side.
- Minimum size: full logo **88px wide (25mm print)**; mark **20px tall (6mm)**. Smaller than that: use the mark alone.
- Placement: top-left on documents and screens. Centred only on the app icon, splash screens and merch.

### Please don't
Rotate or tilt it · change its colours · lose the contrast (e.g. reversed logo on lilac) · retype or capitalise it. Also avoid shadows, outlines, gradients, flipping the mark, or placing it on busy parts of a photo.

## 05 · Voice — a friend who is good at business
- **Plain words** — "Get paid", not "process remittance". "Clients", not "contacts".
- **Warm, not cute** — one light touch per screen. No puns in error messages.
- **Action first** — lead with what to do: "3 invoices are overdue. Send a reminder?"
- **Talk to one person** — "You" and "your clients". Never "users". Global English, local money.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Payment of NGN 1,400,000.00 for invoice #1308 has become past due. | Tara's invoice (₦1.4M) is 2 days late. Send a nudge? |
| Error 402: Transaction declined. | That card was declined. Ask your client to try another card or pay by transfer. |
| No message selected. | Pick a conversation to read it here. |
| Questionnaire · Collection · Session type | Client form · Gallery · Booking |

## 06 · Look & feel
- **Icons:** rounded line icons, 1.75px stroke, 24px grid. Plum or muted ink. Marigold for one celebratory icon at a time. No emoji in the product.
- **Imagery — do:** real creatives and their real work. Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, London, Atlanta. Warm, natural light and candid moments.
- **Imagery — don't:** stock handshakes, empty desks with laptops, or cold blue office scenes.
- **Screenshots:** on lilac or plum grounds with 28px rounded corners.
- **In the product:** every screen leads with what needs doing. Paper and plum carry the layout; marigold marks the one thing to celebrate.
